## Biography — for Ubiquitous

Kenneth R. Passey, known as **“Stretch,”** is an Arizona-based AI software engineer, independent technology builder, entrepreneur, and artist working at the intersection of intelligent systems and human creativity. A U.S. Navy veteran, Kenneth brings an unconventional combination of technical curiosity, creative thinking, and personal resilience to everything he builds.

Through **PulseBridge AI Consulting**, he works on practical AI applications that go beyond conversation to take useful actions: agent-assisted customer communication, lead intake and follow-up, workflow orchestration, software development, research, and business operations. His engineering work spans Python, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, APIs, databases, cloud deployment, and large-language-model integrations.

His portfolio includes **AgentForge OS**, an AI-agent workspace with a policy-based safety layer called PulseGuard; **Pulse Guardian AI**, a tool-using business assistant prototype; and **PulseBridge Mission Control**, a dashboard concept for coordinating multiple AI workflows. He is also exploring **Evograph**, a framework idea for monitoring and improving automated feedback loops. These projects reflect his larger goal: turning scattered AI tools into reliable, coordinated systems that people can actually control.

Kenneth has pursued extensive independent study in artificial intelligence, blockchain technology, and cryptography. His approach is hands-on: learning by designing, building, connecting, testing, and refining real software. Alongside engineering, his background as a visual artist shapes how he thinks about interface design, digital identity, creative AI, and the experience people have with technology.

**His direction in AI is clear:** develop human-centered agent platforms that combine reasoning, automation, memory, secure tool access, and creative capabilities—while keeping people in control of important decisions. He is particularly interested in systems that help individuals and small businesses compete with larger organizations, and in educational products that teach others to build and deploy AI.

Kenneth is **joining forces with Ryan Bashford and the Ubiquitous platform**, bringing his perspective in AI engineering, automation, product development, and digital creativity to a shared effort to make emerging technology more useful and accessible. The exact scope of the collaboration is being defined.
